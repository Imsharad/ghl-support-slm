02

PEFT frontier beyond plain LoRA

this week

The research chatter in early September 2026 is about initialization and geometry of low-rank adapters, not just rank size. DoRA still shows up in applied posts as “better than LoRA.”

NoRA — Normalized Low-Rank Adaptation

Paper arxiv.org/abs/2608.31036. Claim: standard LoRA’s zero-init on B leaves early training dominated by a poorly scaled random A; column-normalize A along the rank dimension so early gradients match full finetuning. NoRA-init (normalize once) captures most gains — no extra params, fully mergeable.

  • Author thread reports wins vs LoRA, DoRA, PiSSA, rsLoRA, OFT on SFT; stronger on RLVR where spectral inits can collapse.
  • Skeptical reply (@zraytam): check whether learning rate was fairly tuned across methods.
  • @ZhengZhan13: gap vs full FT is still about init/scale/normalization, not only rank.

04

Distillation, OPD, and synthetic data

frontier

For custom domains, the loudest cutting-edge thread is teacher→student: synthetic traces from a strong model, then SFT / DPO / RL on the SLM. On-policy distillation (OPD) is the research tip of that spear right now.

On-policy distillation (OPD) — Sep 2026 papers circulating hard

  • One training example is enough? One query recovers ~60–75% of full-data OPD gains; 16 diverse queries ≈ full data via state coverage (arxiv.org/abs/2609.04172). Bottleneck = student absorption rate, not prompt-set size.
  • Sequential beats joint: OPD-then-RL (OPD to expand teacher-support coverage, then GRPO/RLVR to sharpen) beats pure OPD, pure RL, and joint hybrids that mix signals in one step.

Takeaway for a custom-task SLM (e.g. GoHighLevel-style assignment)

  1. Start with a small instruct base (Qwen / Gemma / Phi class, ≤4B).
  2. Build a high-quality task dataset; prefer teacher-generated synthetic traces + hard filters over raw scrape volume.
  3. Train with Unsloth (or LLaMA-Factory) QLoRA; try NoRA-init if you can swap the adapter init.
  4. If the task is verifiable, add GRPO (or DPO from preference pairs). Watch for reward hacking.
  5. If you have a strong teacher, consider OPD then RL rather than mixing distillation and RL in one loss.
  6. Export INT4/GGUF; measure task accuracy vs a large API baseline on your eval set, not general chat benchmarks.
